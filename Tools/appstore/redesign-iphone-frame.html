iPhone store frame — 3 treatments vs the store mask

The App Store clips every iPhone 6.9″ screenshot into a device-shaped card (radius ≈ 14% of width, ~185 px native). The shipped poster is square-cornered — 1.2u mat, hairline inset, UMD flush at the bottom — so the mask eats the frame unevenly at all four corners and the caption row collides with the bottom curve. Each version below is rendered inside that simulated mask, on a product-page-dark shelf, using the real shipped raw captures (wall and keys as the test shots).
