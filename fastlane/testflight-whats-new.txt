NEW SINCE 1.4.0
• SIDE PANEL — on iPad and Vision Pro a confirmed path or web link now opens beside the terminal instead of as a tab; resize it, open another (it replaces the first), or press TAB to move it into a tab. The terminal never changes size.
• TRADITIONAL CHINESE AND JAPANESE — pick per app in Settings → Multiplex → Language (LANGUAGE in Multiplex Settings takes you there).
• ANTIGRAVITY CLI AND HERMES AGENT — both detected on the deck, launchable from New Session, widgets and Shortcuts, each with its own helper chips.
• WHAT'S NEW CARD — the 1.4.1 card opens once after the update; FULL NOTES and Settings → About → What's New keep every release.
