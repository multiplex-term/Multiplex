NEW SINCE 1.5.0
• herdr: Select Text stays inside the pane you pressed on herdr 0.9 and later. A multi-line copy no longer picks up the sidebar's border or drops the pane's right edge, and a zoomed pane selects as the whole pane.
• herdr: long-press text and keep going into a drag to select with herdr itself, the way a mouse drag works in iTerm or Ghostty. herdr keeps the selection inside the pane and puts it on the clipboard when you lift. A long press that doesn't move still opens SELECT / SELECT ALL / PASTE.
• Full screen: three-dot menu → Full Screen hands the window to the terminal; the rails, tabs, and keys step aside, and the agent bar folds to its dot. One button in the corner brings them back.
• herdr: the HRDR panel can now show or hide herdr's sidebar (Toggle Sidebar under Workspaces).
• iPhone Duo: the file viewer's top bar stops short of the clock and Wi-Fi glyphs on the open display held upright, like a terminal's, so CLOSE is no longer hidden under them.
• Single-window mode: a deck you hid with HIDE stays hidden when you rotate between landscape and portrait (iPhone Duo open, Pro Max). It still comes back if you went to the deck in portrait, or when you open the Duo from the closed display.
• Hosts: a host you add, or bind with mpx, now lands at the top of the host list instead of the bottom.

3. In a terminal running an agent, open the three-dot menu → Full Screen. Only the terminal, the agent bar's dot, and one button should remain: top right on iPhone and iPad (it fades after a moment), EXIT at the bottom right on Vision Pro. Type, scroll, and open the agent dot as usual, then press the button; the rails, keys, and agent bar should come back as they were. On iPhone the status bar hides too. On iPhone Duo try it closed (the side column stays), open, as a book, and as a laptop.
