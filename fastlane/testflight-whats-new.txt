CTRL QUICK COMBOS — press CTRL, get C / B

Pressing the rail's CTRL key (or the visionOS cluster's) now raises a
one-line C / B strip over the key while the latch is held: one tap sends
Ctrl+C (interrupt) or Ctrl+B (the tmux prefix) without summoning the
keyboard. Unlatching CTRL, typing any key, or tapping a combo puts it
away.

Worth checking:
- Press CTRL at a shell prompt, tap C: the line aborts with ^C and the
  CTRL latch releases. B likewise (B then a key drives tmux).
- Mash CTRL quickly: the strip must appear/disappear in step with the
  latch every time, never get stuck or skip a beat.
- With an agent running (helper chips above the rail), the strip must sit
  ON TOP of the chip row, tappable, not hidden behind it.
- Latch CTRL and type c on the keyboard instead: same ^C, strip goes away.
- iPad/iPhone: the strip is square chassis (no rounded popover); Vision
  Pro shows a small glass popover over the ornament's CTRL key.
