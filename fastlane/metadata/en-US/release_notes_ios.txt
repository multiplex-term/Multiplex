Multiplex 1.5.2 — find text in the file viewer, and give the iPhone Duo key row the full width of the screen.

• File viewer: press FIND on the bar, or Command-F with a keyboard, and type. Every match is marked, with a count; Return or the arrows step through them. It works on code, diffs, and rendered markdown, and stays open as you pick other files.
• iPhone Duo: the key row runs under the side column on both displays, and the keys at its ends stay clear of the rounded corners. A file viewer or web page tab's bottom bar clears the corners too, and the closed display in landscape loses its grey bands.
• iPhone: locking the keyboard no longer takes keys off the key row. In landscape, ~ and / stay, with RET alongside them.
• iPad: with Display Zoom set to More Space, the terminal no longer draws magnified, cutting off its top row and landing taps off the rows you see.
• Tapping a slash command such as /compact in an agent's input bar no longer offers to open it as a file.
