Multiplex 1.0.1 — fixes for sessions that outlive the lock screen.

• Sessions re-attach themselves after the system suspends the app. Locking the screen kills the app's sockets while tmux keeps running on the host; a tab that lost its connection that way now reconnects on the next foreground instead of parking on a RECONNECT panel you had to press yourself. A session you deliberately exited, detached, or killed stays ended.
• Hardware Shift+Return now inserts a newline in Claude Code, Codex, and Pi composers instead of submitting the prompt. Plain Return still sends.
