Multiplex 1.5 — talk to your agent through the message box, with background noise filtered out and AirPods doing the listening.

• A mic in the message box: tap it beside the send button and talk; words land as you speak, and sending mid-sentence waits for the last words.
• Noise reduction: Settings ▸ Voice input downloads a model that filters background noise out of the microphone before dictation hears it.
• Dictation listens through AirPods or any Bluetooth headset mic when one is connected.
• Hermes Agent is recognized again on hosts with its current installer, and Claude Code's folder-trust question now raises an agent alert.
• A NO TMUX or NO HERDR tile's hint wraps across the tile instead of one character per line.
