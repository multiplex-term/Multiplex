Multiplex 1.5.1 — give the whole window to the terminal, and select text in herdr panes the way a mouse drag does on your desktop.

• Full screen: three-dot menu ▸ Full Screen hands the window to the terminal. The rails, tabs, keys, and ornaments step aside and the agent bar folds to its dot; EXIT in the corner brings them back.
• herdr: on herdr 0.9 and later, Select Text stays inside the pane you pressed. Multi-line copies no longer pick up the sidebar's border or lose the pane's right edge.
• herdr: long-press text and keep going into a drag to let herdr select it, the way a mouse drag works in iTerm or Ghostty. Lifting copies it to the clipboard.
• herdr: the HRDR panel can show or hide herdr's sidebar (Toggle Sidebar under Workspaces).
• A host's SHELL and three-dot buttons on the deck answer a pinch anywhere on them; one near their top edge used to do nothing.
• Smaller Text and Larger Text leave the three-dot menu open, so you can keep stepping the size.
• A host you add, or bind with mpx, lands at the top of the host list.
