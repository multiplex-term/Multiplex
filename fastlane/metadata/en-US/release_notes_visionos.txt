Multiplex 1.5.2 — find text in the file viewer, and lock the keyboard closed.

• File viewer: press FIND on the bar, or Command-F with a keyboard, and type. Every match is marked, with a count; Return or the arrows step through them. It works on code, diffs, and rendered markdown, and stays open as you pick other files.
• Lock the keyboard closed: hold the keyboard key under the window, or choose Lock Keyboard Closed in the three-dot menu. Selecting the terminal no longer brings up the keyboard, and the other keys under the window keep working.
• Tapping a slash command such as /compact in an agent's input bar no longer offers to open it as a file.
