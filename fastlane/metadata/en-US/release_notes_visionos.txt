Multiplex 1.0.1 — fixes for sessions that outlive a suspended app, and a shorter terminal ornament.

• Sessions re-attach themselves after the system suspends the app. Backgrounding Multiplex can kill its sockets while tmux keeps running on the host; a tab that lost its connection that way now reconnects when you come back instead of parking on a RECONNECT panel you had to press yourself. A session you deliberately exited, detached, or killed stays ended.
• Hardware Shift+Return now inserts a newline in Claude Code, Codex, and Pi composers instead of submitting the prompt. Plain Return still sends.
• The terminal window's key cluster moved onto the window bar's own row: ESC, CTRL, and TAB sit to its left, the arrows, Return, and the keyboard toggle to its right. The ornament is one row shorter, and the agent helper strip above it is slimmer.
